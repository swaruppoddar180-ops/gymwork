GYM RULES — MANDATORY FOR EVERYONE

Train Hard. Respect The Floor.

1

Timing only: 6 AM–12 PM & 4 PM–11 PM (Mon–Sat). No entry during break (12–4 PM). Sunday CLOSED.

2

Re-rack weights after every set. No dropping dumbbells.

3
